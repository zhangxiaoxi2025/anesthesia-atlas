Local adaptation (2026-10-24): selected named right-side neck and central skeletal meshes from four pinned source FBX files, applied original matrixWorld and uniform 0.01 centimeter-to-meter conversion, deduplicated exact position+normal tuples, serialized positions to eight decimal places. No source anatomy was moved, fitted, connected, split by assumed spinal level, or procedurally posed. Whole selected meshes are preserved; a right-neck display ROI is only a view aid. Original axillary-module geometry is not modified.

Source label Roots of brachial plexus is retained as provenance for a merged, unsegmented proximal plexus mesh. This export does not identify individual C5/C6/C7 ventral rami and does not substitute intrathecal spinal root meshes. No phrenic nerve, fascia, injection space, patient-specific ultrasound registration or clinically calibrated scanning plane is generated.

Only named peripheral nerve and related muscular/vascular/skeletal reference meshes are exported. Brain white matter, cranial nerves, inner ear and kidney third-party examples are not included.

Pinned URLs, source IDs, hashes, geometry bounds, source inventory, adaptations and limitations are in manifest.json. Software integrity checks do not constitute clinical anatomical validation or approval of a block technique.
